The idea in one minute

Just Fiends is one function generator copied six times. All six copies share the same TIME, RAMP and CURVE. The only thing that makes them different is INTONE, which sets how fast each channel runs compared with the first one (IDENTITY).

  • INTONE at noon: all six are identical.
  • INTONE fully clockwise: channel N runs N times faster. That is the harmonic series: in Sound range one note becomes a big chord; in Shape range one LFO becomes six related LFOs.
  • INTONE fully anticlockwise: channel N runs N times slower (subharmonics, long divisions).
  • In between, the ratios are not whole numbers, so the channels drift in and out of phase. This is where the Autechre-style slow evolving movement comes from.

The display

  • Top line: the Range and Mode, then the name of what the module is doing now (for example LFOS or RUN: VOLLEY) and a one-line explanation.
  • Seven lanes: the six channels and the MIX, each with its ratio to IDENTITY and its rate. A dot next to a lane means that channel's gate is high.
  • Shape range scrolls (newest on the right). Sound range shows a steady scope of two IDENTITY cycles.
  • Right: one cycle of the current RAMP and CURVE shape, plus read-outs. Hover any knob or jack for a tooltip that explains it in the current mode.

Run modes

Press ON (or patch RUN IN). The RUN voltage (knob plus jack) now has a job that depends on Range and Mode. The display names the mode in green.

NameRange / ModeWhat RUN does
SHIFTShape / TransientMoves the retrigger point. 0 V: any trigger restarts the slope. 5 V: triggers are ignored until the slope has finished, so it becomes a clock divider whose division depends on TIME.
STRATAShape / SustainSix slew limiters follow the RUN voltage (0 to 8 V), each at its own speed from INTONE. RAMP sets rise against fall speed. A low gate freezes that channel (track and hold); with nothing patched they all track.
VOLLEYShape / CycleEach trigger fires a burst of cycles, then stops. RUN sets how many: 1 at 0 V up to 16 at 5 V. Ratchets.
SPILLSound / TransientEach trigger spills a burst of tone. RUN sets the length, from 5 ms (a click) to about 2 s.
PLUMESound / SustainTriggers pluck the voices like a low pass gate: a fast attack, then a decay that also darkens the tone towards a sine. RUN sets the decay, from 20 ms to about 4 s. Trigger voltage sets how hard the pluck is.
FLOOMSound / CycleEach voice frequency-modulates the next one in a ring (6N modulates IDENTITY). RUN sets the depth. Bright, metallic, chaotic at the top.

Poly pitch and Geode (right-click menu)

Poly pitch (synth mode)

Each channel reads its own channel of the TIME input, and INTONE is ignored. Patch a poly V/OCT from a MIDI to CV module into TIME and its poly GATE into POLY TRIG, and Just Fiends is a six-voice synth. Use Sound / Sustain for organ-like voices, or Sound / Sustain with Run on for Plume plucks. The hardware gets this mode from a Teletype over i2c; here it is one menu tick.

Geode (rhythm machine)

IDENTITY becomes a clock input. Each channel plays its own rhythm locked to it, at INTONE's ratio: with INTONE fully clockwise, channel N plays N hits per clock; anticlockwise, channel N plays once every N clocks. Other trigger jacks are ignored.

In-between INTONE settings give non-whole ratios that drift. Realign every (also in the menu) sets how many clocks pass before all channels snap back together: 1 keeps it tight, 16 lets them wander for four bars. The Mode switch still applies, so Geode in Sound / Sustain with Run on plays plucked rhythms.

Starter patches: Detroit meets Autechre

These use VCV Fundamental modules plus your other plugins where helpful. Cable lists are in order, so you can rebuild each one from scratch.

1. Strings of Life chord pad

SOUNDSUSTAIN

One note becomes a Detroit string chord, because INTONE clockwise stacks harmonics.

  1. MIDI to CV (or your sequencer) V/OCT → Just Fiends TIME V/OCT.
  2. MIDI to CV GATE → IDENTITY (it normals to all six).
  3. Just Fiends MIX → VCF in; VCF out → a reverb, then Audio out.
  4. LFO (0.05 Hz, sine) → Just Fiends INTONE CV, amt at 15%.

Settings: TIME noon (tune with V/OCT), INTONE fully clockwise, RAMP 1 o'clock, CURVE 3 o'clock (towards sine), VCF cutoff low with a little resonance.

Variations: INTONE at 2 o'clock gives an out-of-tune, shimmering chord that beats slowly. Turn on Run (Plume) with RUN at 1 V for stabs instead of a pad.

2. Six-arm LFO bank for steering an arrangement

SHAPECYCLE

The answer to loop fatigue: six related LFOs that only line up again after a long time.

  1. IDENTITY → filter cutoff of your main loop (through an attenuator).
  2. 2N → delay feedback or reverb mix.
  3. 3N → Marbles-style randomness amount, or a sequencer's probability CV.
  4. 4N → hat VCA level.
  5. 5N → a pan or wavefolder.
  6. 6N → RAMP CV of this same Just Fiends, amt 30% (it reshapes itself).

Settings: TIME fully anticlockwise to 9 o'clock (slow), INTONE 1 o'clock (non-whole ratios), RAMP noon, CURVE 2 o'clock.

Variations: INTONE fully anticlockwise makes 6N take six times longer than IDENTITY, which suits a 2 to 5 minute arc. Send a trigger to IDENTITY at the start of each 32-bar section to realign everything on purpose.

3. Volley ratchet hats

SHAPECYCLERUN: VOLLEY
  1. Clock (16ths) → a Bernoulli gate or probability module → Just Fiends IDENTITY.
  2. Random (sample and hold, 0 to 5 V, clocked by the same 16ths) → RUN IN.
  3. IDENTITY → VCA CV for a noise hi-hat; 3N → VCA CV for a second, brighter hat.

Settings: TIME around 2 o'clock (one burst cycle about one 64th), INTONE fully clockwise, RAMP fully anticlockwise, CURVE 1 o'clock, RUN knob 0.

Variations: Fixed RUN at 1.3 V gives exactly 5 hits. Switch to Shape / Transient (Shift) and the same patch becomes a clock divider instead.

4. Plume bassline

SOUNDSUSTAINRUN: PLUMEPOLY PITCH
  1. Sequencer or Hapax pitch (mono is fine) → TIME V/OCT.
  2. Euclidean gates (for example Seuperclidean) → IDENTITY.
  3. IDENTITY → a saturator or drive, then mixer. 2N → same mixer, low level.
  4. Slow LFO → RUN IN (0 to 3 V) so decays open and close over 16 bars.

Settings: TIME one octave down (about 10 o'clock), INTONE fully clockwise (2N is an octave up), RAMP 11 o'clock, CURVE noon (the pluck darkens towards sine on its own). Right-click: keep Poly pitch off for this one.

Variations: Turn Poly pitch on, patch a poly MIDI to CV, and you have six-voice plucked chords.

5. Geode drum machine

SHAPETRANSIENTGEODE

Six interlocking rhythms from one clock, that slowly fall out of and back into line.

  1. Clock (quarter notes, 128 BPM) → IDENTITY. Right-click → Geode on, Realign every 4 clocks.
  2. IDENTITY → kick trigger. 2N → clap or snare VCA. 4N → closed hat VCA. 6N → a shaker or a percussive filter ping.
  3. 3N and 5N → modulation (filter cutoff, FM depth) for accents in the same rhythm.

Settings: TIME 2 o'clock (short envelopes), INTONE fully clockwise for a straight grid, then nudge to 3 o'clock for broken, Autechre-like timing. RAMP fully anticlockwise, CURVE 1 o'clock.

Variations: Put a slow random voltage into INTONE CV with amt 20% so the rhythm mutates by itself. Realign every 16 for longer drifting phrases.

6. Strata chord glides

SHAPESUSTAINRUN: STRATA
  1. Sequencer pitch CV (0 to 8 V) → RUN IN.
  2. Each output IDENTITY to 6N → the V/OCT of six oscillators (or a poly oscillator from POLY OUT).
  3. Mix the oscillators, then a filter and reverb.

Settings: TIME 1 o'clock, INTONE fully anticlockwise (6N glides six times slower), RAMP noon.

Each new note starts as one pitch and smears into the next one voice by voice. Patch gates into individual triggers to freeze some voices and build chords from held notes.

7. Floom drone

SOUNDCYCLERUN: FLOOM
  1. Very slow LFO (0.02 Hz) → RUN IN.
  2. MIX → a VCA, then delay and reverb.

Settings: TIME 9 o'clock (low), INTONE 1 o'clock, CURVE fully clockwise (sine), RAMP noon. As the RUN voltage rises, the ring of FM blooms from a soft chord into metallic noise and back.

Moving it to your hardware rig

Everything above transfers to the real module in your case, with the same knob positions as starting points.

  • Pitch: Metropolix or Hapax pitch into TIME (V/OCT). One note plus INTONE clockwise gives the chord in patch 1.
  • Triggers: Pam's New Workout clock outputs into IDENTITY and 4N to split the normals. Marbles t outputs for random gates. DROID logic (for example a gate that only passes every third bar) into individual channels.
  • Run: on hardware there is no RUN knob or button; patching the RUN jack is what turns it on. Use a Pam's output set to a fixed voltage, or a DROID P2B8 fader, as your RUN knob.
  • Processing: outputs into Three Sisters (as a filter bank for patch 1), Cold Mac (crossfade two channels with SURVEY), Just Friends' own outputs into Mangrove for formant pings.
  • Ableton: Live as clock through CV Tools out of the disting NT, into IDENTITY in Geode-like setups, or into Pam's which then feeds Just Friends.

Poly pitch and Geode need a Teletype or crow on the hardware (over i2c). In VCV they are menu items, so you can learn the ideas now.

How Just Fiends differs from the hardware

  • Added for learning: the display, mode-aware tooltips, trigger buttons, the RUN knob and ON button, CV amount trimmers, poly in and out, and a velocity option in the menu.
  • The six Run modes follow the hardware's ideas and names, but the details are this module's own versions, not exact copies. In particular, Spill (timed bursts) and Floom (a ring of FM) are interpretations.
  • MIX here is the highest slope in Shape range and a soft-clipped sum in Sound range.
  • Geode is driven by a clock at IDENTITY and INTONE, not by Teletype commands.
