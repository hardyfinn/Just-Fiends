The idea in one minute

Just Fiends is one function generator copied six times. All six copies share the same TIME, RAMP and CURVE. The only thing that makes them different is INTONE, which sets how fast each channel runs compared with the first one (IDENTITY).

  • INTONE at noon: all six are identical.
  • INTONE fully clockwise: channel N runs N times faster. That is the harmonic series: in Sound range one note becomes a big chord; in Shape range one LFO becomes six related LFOs.
  • INTONE fully anticlockwise: channel N runs N times slower (subharmonics, long divisions).
  • In between, the ratios are not whole numbers, so the channels drift in and out of phase. This is where the Autechre-style slow evolving movement comes from.

Above: INTONE fully clockwise. Top line is IDENTITY, then 2N to 6N, each running one step faster.

The display

  • Top line: the Range and Mode, then the name of what the module is doing now (for example LFOS or RUN: VOLLEY) and a one-line explanation.
  • Seven lanes: the six channels and the MIX, each with its ratio to IDENTITY and its rate. A dot next to a lane means that channel's gate is high.
  • Shape range scrolls (newest on the right). Sound range shows a steady scope of two IDENTITY cycles.
  • Right: one cycle of the current RAMP and CURVE shape, plus read-outs. Hover any knob or jack for a tooltip that explains it in the current mode.

Run modes

Press ON (or patch RUN IN). The RUN voltage (knob plus jack) now has a job that depends on Range and Mode. The display names the mode in green.

NameRange / ModeWhat RUN does
SHIFTShape / TransientMoves the retrigger point. -5 V: any trigger restarts. 0 V: triggers are taken once the rise is done. +5 V: only once the slope has finished (normal Transient). A retrigger restarts from 0 V.
STRATAShape / SustainARSR envelopes: a gate rises to the top, falls to a sustain level set by RUN (-5 V to +5 V gives 0 to 8 V, before CURVE), holds while the gate is high, then falls to 0. Hold a gate high in 6N and changing RUN makes six slew limiters, each at its own speed from INTONE.
VOLLEYShape / CycleEach trigger fires a burst of envelopes, then stops. RUN sets how many, latched at the trigger: below -4 V none (choked), 1 at -4 V, 6 at 0 V, 36 at +5 V. Ratchets.
SPILLSound / TransientNo cables needed: IDENTITY runs free and its end of cycle fires 2N to 6N as impulses. INTONE clockwise: impulse trains (like Mangrove FORMANT). Anticlockwise: subharmonics and split tones. RUN sets when a busy slope can be retriggered (-5 V any time, +5 V only when done).
PLUMESound / SustainSix free-running oscillators through low pass gates. Triggers pluck them; gates hold them open like piano keys. RUN 0 V is a classic vactrol; positive is snappier; negative is longer and touch-sensitive (short triggers play softer).
FLOOMSound / CycleTwo-operator FM: each voice gets its own sine modulator. RUN sets the ratio (-5 V half, 0 V the same, +5 V double). The FM knob sets the depth (clockwise all equally, anticlockwise by channel number) and the FM jack becomes a depth CV.

Poly pitch and Geode (right-click menu)

Poly pitch (synth mode)

Each channel reads its own channel of the TIME input, and INTONE is ignored. Patch a poly V/OCT from a MIDI to CV module into TIME and its poly GATE into POLY TRIG, and Just Fiends is a six-voice synth. Use Sound / Sustain for organ-like voices, or Sound / Sustain with Run on for Plume plucks. The hardware gets this mode from a Teletype over i2c; here it is one menu tick.

Geode (rhythm machine)

IDENTITY becomes a clock input. Each channel plays its own rhythm locked to it, at INTONE's ratio: with INTONE fully clockwise, channel N plays N hits per clock; anticlockwise, channel N plays once every N clocks. Other trigger jacks are ignored.

In-between INTONE settings give non-whole ratios that drift. Realign every (also in the menu) sets how many clocks pass before all channels snap back together: 1 keeps it tight, 16 lets them wander for four bars. The Mode switch still applies, so Geode in Sound / Sustain with Run on plays plucked rhythms.

Starter patches: Detroit meets Autechre

These use VCV Fundamental modules plus your other plugins where helpful. Cable lists are in order, so you can rebuild each one from scratch.

1. Strings of Life chord pad

SOUNDSUSTAINRUN: PLUME

One note becomes a Detroit string chord, because INTONE clockwise stacks harmonics.

  1. MIDI to CV (or your sequencer) V/OCT → Just Fiends TIME V/OCT.
  2. MIDI to CV GATE → 6N (it normals to all six).
  3. Just Fiends MIX → VCF in; VCF out → a reverb, then Audio out.
  4. LFO (0.05 Hz, sine) → Just Fiends INTONE CV, amt at 15%.

Settings: Run ON with the RUN knob at -2 V (slow, soft low pass gates that stay open while the gate is held), TIME noon (tune with V/OCT), INTONE fully clockwise, RAMP 1 o'clock, CURVE 3 o'clock (towards sine), VCF cutoff low with a little resonance.

Variations: INTONE at 2 o'clock gives an out-of-tune, shimmering chord that beats slowly. Short triggers instead of gates, with RUN at +2 V, give stabs instead of a pad.

2. Six-arm LFO bank for steering an arrangement

SHAPECYCLE

The answer to loop fatigue: six related LFOs that only line up again after a long time.

  1. IDENTITY → filter cutoff of your main loop (through an attenuator).
  2. 2N → delay feedback or reverb mix.
  3. 3N → Marbles-style randomness amount, or a sequencer's probability CV.
  4. 4N → hat VCA level.
  5. 5N → a pan or wavefolder.
  6. 6N → RAMP CV of this same Just Fiends, amt 30% (it reshapes itself).

Settings: TIME fully anticlockwise to 9 o'clock (slow), INTONE 1 o'clock (non-whole ratios), RAMP noon, CURVE 2 o'clock.

Variations: INTONE fully anticlockwise makes 6N take six times longer than IDENTITY, which suits a 2 to 5 minute arc. Send a trigger to 6N at the start of each 32-bar section to realign all six on purpose.

3. Volley ratchet hats

SHAPECYCLERUN: VOLLEY
  1. Clock (16ths) → a Bernoulli gate or probability module → Just Fiends IDENTITY.
  2. Random (sample and hold, ±2 V, clocked by the same 16ths) → RUN IN.
  3. IDENTITY → VCA CV for a noise hi-hat; 3N → VCA CV for a second, brighter hat.

Settings: TIME around 2 o'clock (one burst cycle about one 64th), INTONE fully clockwise, RAMP fully anticlockwise, CURVE 1 o'clock, RUN knob -2 V, so each hit plays 1 to 6 times.

Variations: Fixed RUN at -0.8 V gives exactly 5 hits; below -4 V it is choked. Switch to Shape / Transient (Shift) and the same patch becomes a clock divider instead.

4. Plume bassline

SOUNDSUSTAINRUN: PLUMEPOLY PITCH
  1. Sequencer or Hapax pitch (mono is fine) → TIME V/OCT.
  2. Euclidean gates (for example Seuperclidean) → 2N (it normals to IDENTITY too).
  3. IDENTITY → a saturator or drive, then mixer. 2N → same mixer, low level.
  4. Slow LFO → RUN IN (±3 V) so plucks get longer and shorter over 16 bars.

Settings: TIME one octave down (about 10 o'clock), INTONE fully clockwise (2N is an octave up), RAMP 11 o'clock, CURVE noon (the pluck darkens towards sine on its own). Right-click: keep Poly pitch off for this one.

Variations: Turn Poly pitch on, patch a poly MIDI to CV, and you have six-voice plucked chords.

5. Geode drum machine

SHAPETRANSIENTGEODE

Six interlocking rhythms from one clock, that slowly fall out of and back into line.

  1. Clock (quarter notes, 128 BPM) → IDENTITY. Right-click → Geode on, Realign every 4 clocks.
  2. IDENTITY → kick trigger. 2N → clap or snare VCA. 4N → closed hat VCA. 6N → a shaker or a percussive filter ping.
  3. 3N and 5N → modulation (filter cutoff, FM depth) for accents in the same rhythm.

Settings: TIME 2 o'clock (short envelopes), INTONE fully clockwise for a straight grid, then nudge to 3 o'clock for broken, Autechre-like timing. RAMP fully anticlockwise, CURVE 1 o'clock.

Variations: Put a slow random voltage into INTONE CV with amt 20% so the rhythm mutates by itself. Realign every 16 for longer drifting phrases.

6. Strata chord glides

SHAPESUSTAINRUN: STRATA
  1. A steady 5 V (an offset module, or a gate held high) → 6N, so all six sit in their sustain stage.
  2. Sequencer CV (-5 to +5 V) → RUN IN.
  3. Each output IDENTITY to 6N → a quantizer, then the V/OCT of six oscillators (or a poly quantizer and oscillator from POLY OUT). RUN is not 1 V/oct, so the quantizer keeps the notes in a scale.
  4. Mix the oscillators, then a filter and reverb.

Settings: TIME 1 o'clock, INTONE fully anticlockwise (6N glides six times slower), RAMP noon.

Each new note starts as one pitch and smears into the next one voice by voice, arriving at the same note at different times. Patch separate gates into individual triggers and the voices drop to 0 V when theirs goes low.

7. Floom drone

SOUNDCYCLERUN: FLOOM
  1. Very slow LFO (0.02 Hz, ±5 V) → RUN IN.
  2. MIX → a VCA, then delay and reverb.

Settings: TIME 9 o'clock (low), INTONE 1 o'clock, CURVE fully clockwise (sine), RAMP noon, FM knob 2 o'clock. The LFO sweeps each modulator from half to double its voice's pitch, so the chord passes through clangy and sweet ratios. FM fully clockwise, RUN below 0 and RAMP at either end gives noise.

Moving it to your hardware rig

Since 2.5.0 the engine follows the Just Friends Technical Map (normalling, retrigger rules, FM, MIX and the six Run modes), so the patches above transfer to the real module with the same knob positions as starting points. On hardware, use a fixed voltage into RUN instead of the RUN knob.

  • Pitch: Metropolix or Hapax pitch into TIME (V/OCT). One note plus INTONE clockwise gives the chord in patch 1.
  • Triggers: Pam's New Workout clock outputs into 6N and 3N to split the normals (6N fires 4N to 6N, 3N fires IDENTITY to 3N). Marbles t outputs for random gates. DROID logic (for example a gate that only passes every third bar) into individual channels.
  • Run: on hardware there is no RUN knob or button; patching the RUN jack is what turns it on. Use a Pam's output set to a fixed voltage, or a DROID P2B8 fader, as your RUN knob.
  • Processing: outputs into Three Sisters (as a filter bank for patch 1), Cold Mac (crossfade two channels with SURVEY), Just Friends' own outputs into Mangrove for formant pings.
  • Ableton: Live as clock through CV Tools out of the disting NT, into 6N to fire all six, or into Pam's which then feeds Just Friends.

Poly pitch and Geode need a Teletype or crow on the hardware (over i2c). In VCV they are menu items, so you can learn the ideas now.

How Just Fiends differs from the hardware

  • Added for learning: the display, mode-aware tooltips, trigger buttons, the RUN knob and ON button, CV amount trimmers, poly in and out, and a velocity option in the menu (off by default; the hardware has no velocity).
  • 2.5.0 was checked against the Technical Map. Still this module's own guesses: the TIME ranges, the shape of the INTONE knob between its ends, the Plume vactrol times and the Floom FM depth, which the map does not give in numbers.
  • Geode is driven by a clock at IDENTITY and INTONE, not by Teletype commands.

Weird Sisters: three linked filters

Weird Sisters is one filter copied three times, the same idea as Just Fiends. All three share FREQ and QUALITY. SPAN pushes LOW below and HIGH above the centre, by the same number of octaves each way.

Mangrave: a formant oscillator with PPM built in

Mangrave follows the Mangrove technical map. A triangle core oscillator gives the SQUARE output. Every rising edge of the core fires the impulse: a little envelope that rises to the top and falls to the bottom, then rests until the next trigger. The impulse goes through AIR (a VCA and then an overdrive) to the FORMANT output. There is no filter: the tone comes from the impulse's length and shape.

The scope shows it: grey is the core, amber marks when the impulse is rising, green is the FORMANT output.

ControlWhat it doesTry
PITCH, FINE + V/OCTThe core pitch, middle C at noon. FINE covers one octave.

FORMANT + CV, amtThe impulse length. Clockwise = shorter: brighter, with rests between impulses (think of it as a high pass). Anticlockwise = longer. The impulse ignores the core while it is rising, so once its rise lasts longer than a core cycle it skips cycles and the pitch drops to 1/2, 1/3, 1/4: the undertone series. A core edge that lands while it is falling turns it round, which gives split tones (multiphonics) between the steps. Outputs SQUARE, FORMANTThe core, and the impulses through AIR.

The PPM section

Newer Mangroves come with PPM, a 2 HP expander that stands for "Patch Programmable Mangrove". It opens up the inner workings. Here it is built into the right-hand side of the same panel.

PartWhat it does
Mode switchIMPULSE: classic Mangrove, the core fires each impulse. SYNC: the impulse cycles on its own and the core soft-syncs it (a restart only while it is falling), which gives an inverted Mangrove sound. CYCLE: the impulse is a second free-running oscillator. FORMANT sets its pitch and BARREL its shape, and only the TRIGGER jack syncs it.
TRIGGER in + FIREReplaces the core as what fires the impulse. Clock it with FORMANT low and CONSTANT FORMANT for fast envelopes, or feed it another oscillator to use Mangrave as a waveshaper. FIRE does it by hand.
TO AIR inAdds any signal into the AIR stage next to the impulse, so AIR's VCA and overdrive shape it too.
SINE, SAWThe core as a sine and a saw, alongside SQUARE. SINE is the one for through-zero FM.
PWMHigh while the impulse is rising. BARREL sweeps its width.
HOLLOWFlips on every impulse, so it is a square at half the impulse rate. It follows FORMANT's undertones, with odd harmonics like a bass clarinet. Layer it under FORMANT.

PPM patch ideas (after the PPM manual)

  • Quadricycle: HOLLOW → Mangrave's own V/OCT, CONSTANT FORMANT, PPM CYCLE, FORMANT at minimum. It steps through four tones by itself.
  • Get complex: FORMANT out → FM in, CONSTANT FORMANT, PPM CYCLE. Bell-like through-zero FM.
  • Detune party: SINE or SAW → TO AIR, with the impulse (PPM CYCLE) tuned near the core. Slow beating between the two.
  • Over/under: PWM → BARREL CV in PPM CYCLE. Turning FORMANT up gives overtones, down gives undertones.
  • Waveshaper: another oscillator (or another Mangrave's FORMANT) → TRIGGER in PPM IMPULSE, for stacked undertones.

The trio together: three starter patches

8. Talking bass

MANGRAVEWEIRD SISTERS
  1. Sequencer pitch → Mangrave V/OCT; a Just Fiends envelope (Shape / Transient, fired by the gates) → Mangrave AIR CV.
  2. Mangrave FORMANT out → Weird Sisters ALL in.
  3. Weird Sisters ALL out → mixer.
  4. A slow LFO (Just Fiends 4N in Shape / Cycle) → Weird Sisters FREQ V/OCT.

Settings: Mangrave PITCH 10 o'clock, CONSTANT FORMANT, FORMANT noon, BARREL 10 o'clock, AIR at 0 (the envelope opens it). Weird Sisters FORMANT mode, SPAN 11 o'clock, QUALITY 1 o'clock.

Variations: switch Mangrave to CONSTANT WAVE for a steadier, more synth-like bass. Turn QUALITY anticlockwise for hollow, phased vowels.

9. Three-band Detroit chord

JUST FIENDSWEIRD SISTERS
  1. Just Fiends chord (patch 1: Sound / Sustain, INTONE fully clockwise) MIX → Weird Sisters ALL in.
  2. Weird Sisters LOW, CENTRE, HIGH → three mixer channels, panned left, centre, right.
  3. Just Fiends in a second instance, Shape / Cycle, slow: 2N → Weird Sisters SPAN CV, 3N → FREQ V/OCT.

Settings: Weird Sisters CROSSOVER, SPAN 1 o'clock, QUALITY 1 o'clock. The chord's harmonics move between the three bands and across the stereo field as the LFOs drift.

10. Singing sisters

WEIRD SISTERS
  1. Nothing into the audio inputs. Sequencer pitch → FREQ V/OCT.
  2. A short trigger or clicks (Just Fiends Sound / Transient) → Weird Sisters ALL in to ping the filters.
  3. LOW, CENTRE, HIGH → mixer.

Settings: FORMANT mode. QUALITY 3 o'clock or more (they sing on their own) or about 2:30 (they ring after each ping, like tuned percussion). SPAN sets the interval: just before noon (one octave, shown on the display) puts LOW and HIGH an octave each side, so you get three octaves of the same note. The display shows the three peaks.

Weird Sisters and Mangrave on the hardware

  • The real Three Sisters and Mangrove in your case have the same main controls (FREQ, SPAN, QUALITY, MODE; PITCH, FINE, FM, FORMANT, BARREL, AIR, CONSTANT, SYNC, plus the PPM switch and jacks), so these patches carry over as starting points.
  • Added for learning: the displays, tooltips, CV amount trimmers, the FIRE button, and polyphony. On the hardware, FM INDEX is a CV input only; here there is also a knob.
  • These are this plugin's own models built from the hardware's ideas, not circuit copies. Since 2.5.0 both follow their technical maps closely (block structure, SPAN crossing, dry/wet QUALITY, ALL in, multiphonics, AC-coupled FM). Still interpretations: Weird Sisters' exact ranges and gain staging, Mangrave's overdrive curve, and PPM details (the exact sync behaviour and the HOLLOW divider).
